# Fire Emblem: Blade & Stone — Design Bible

*A Fire Emblem 8 (Sacred Stones) buildfile hack that brings the full casts of FE7 and FE8 together into one original story.*
Working document. Everything here can change. The **Premise**, **Tone** and **Rules of the Crossover** sections are the load-bearing walls.

---

## 1. Premise

In the same year, on two continents, two wars ended.

- In **Elibe**, Eliwood, Hector and Lyn killed Nergal on the Dread Isle of **Valor** and closed the **Dragons' Gate** for good.
- In **Magvel**, Eirika and Ephraim destroyed the Demon King **Fomortiis**. The five Sacred Stones were gone, shattered in the course of the war.

Nobody on either shore knew the other shore existed in any real sense. The Gray Sea between them was too wide and too cruel for sailors.

**The truth:** the Dragons' Gate and the Sacred Stones were two ends of the *same seal*. A thousand years ago, when humans drove the dragons out of Elibe in the **Scouring**, the dragons were not banished to some peaceful elsewhere. They were cast into a **realm between worlds**, a seam of nothing under the sea. Over the centuries, many of them went mad. That madness, pooling and pressing against the weakest point of the seal, leaked into Magvel and took a shape Magvel's people named **the Demon King**. The Sacred Stones were not only prisons for Fomortiis. They were anchors that held the seam shut.

One year ago, *both ends of the seal were broken or slammed shut in the same season.* The Gate was closed with nothing to relieve the pressure, and the Stones were gone with nothing left to anchor it. **The seam is opening.** It opens first where the old anchors were: at the ruined Stone shrines of Magvel, and at the Dragons' Gate. Then it opens in the open sea between.

> **Core idea:** *The heroes' victories caused this crisis.* Nobody did anything wrong, which is exactly what makes it hard.

## 2. Tone

**Serious, with real stakes. Never grimdark.**

- The world is in danger and choices cost something. People we love from both games get hurt, and the *echoes* of the dead hit hard.
- **No gratuitous deaths of playable characters.** Loss comes from the past (echoes, memory, what the wars already took), not from killing off units for shock.
- **Warmth and comedy live in the gaps**: base conversations, supports, and the moments right after a hard-won fight. Two casts of veterans meeting their mirror images is funny, so let it be. Good examples: Sain meets Forde, Kent meets Kyle, Serra meets L'Arachel, and Hector meets Ephraim (a table gets broken).
- Villains are *people with reasons*. The main antagonist is right about history.
- Reference register: the Lyn/Eliwood-era FE7 script, and TMGC's sincerity. Not Fates-style gag writing, not Game of Thrones.

## 3. Rules of the Crossover

1. **Time:** about one year after the ending of both games. Everyone is a *veteran*. Hector is Marquess of Ostia (Uther is dead). Ephraim is King of Renais. The Black Fang is broken.
2. **Geography:** Elibe and Magvel share one world, separated by the **Gray Sea**. **Valor** (the Dread Isle) sits at its eastern edge, and it is where the seam is widest. Crossing is possible but deadly, until the story makes it necessary.
3. **Canon endings:** FE7 = Eliwood/Ninian paired ending (Ninian stayed; **Nils went through the Gate**). FE8 = both routes happened, and all recruitable units survived. Orson died.
4. **No time travel, no summoning.** Everything that happens follows from the seal.
5. **Echoes:** The seam carries *fragments* of the dead. They are not resurrections, and they cannot be saved. An echo is a piece of someone, often the piece that mattered most to them. Echoes can speak, fight and remember, but they fade. **We never cheapen a canon death by undoing it.**
6. **Two dragon girls:** **Ninian** (Elibe) and **Myrrh** (Magvel) are the emotional spine of the dragon plot. Since the Gate closed, Ninian has been *fading*, because a dragon cannot live long in a world sealed away from her kind. The antagonist offers her survival.

## 4. The Antagonist: Ysolde, the Last Archivist

**Ysolde of Arcadia**, a human scholar raised in the hidden village of Arcadia, where humans and dragons live together.

- She watched Arcadia's dragons begin to fade the day the Gate closed. Her adoptive dragon mother, Seren, is dying.
- In Arcadia's archive she found what the Scouring really was: a *genocide*, started by humans, and the exile that followed.
- **Her goal:** open the seam completely so the exiled dragons can come home. *She is right about the past.* She is willing to let Elibe and Magvel drown in the madness that comes through with them, because she believes the madness will heal once the dragons are home. It won't. Not fast enough.
- She is patient, kind to her followers, and genuinely moved by the heroes. She never gloats. Her line to Eliwood: *"You closed a door on a thousand years of screaming and called it peace."*

**Her allies (working):**
- **Brother Maelis** (Magvel): an excommunicated Rausten priest who reads the Stones' shattering as a divine sign. He leads **the Unwritten**, a cult of people who lost everything in the war and want it *all* to end differently.
- **King Desmond of Bern** (Elibe, background): funds Ysolde's "archaeology" on Valor because he wants what comes through. Canon-safe, since he survives until FE6.
- **Kaelen Reed** (Elibe): a Black Fang splinter leader who hunts Nino and Jaffar. He's personal and petty, and makes a good mid-act foil.

**The real final threat is not Ysolde.** It is the **Hollow Throng**: the madness of the exiled dragons given form, the same force Magvel called Fomortiis. Ysolde has to *see* it to understand. The ending is about **repairing the seal differently**, with a way through that the dragons can use. It is not about killing dragons.

## 5. The Third Option: Arcadia

Arcadia proves that humans and dragons can share a world. The story's answer to "seal them forever" versus "let them flood back" is **a door with a keeper**. The final seal is rebuilt with Ninian and Myrrh as its keys, so the way home stays open *and* the madness can be held back and eventually healed. Nils, who went through the Gate a year ago, has been holding a small light inside the seam all this time.

## 7. Structure

About 30 main chapters, a final chapter, and 8 side-party chapters, with free-roam **interludes** between acts in the TMGC style. Early on, chapters alternate between continents so deploy counts stay small (8–12) and each cast is introduced properly.

### Act II — The Crossing (Ch. 11–21)
The allied armies meet at sea. Ship maps, island maps, Arcadia. At the midpoint, Ninian learns she is fading and Ysolde offers to save her.

Joining units: Jehanna side party (Joshua, Gerik, Tethys, Marisa), Caer Pelyn (Saleh, Ewan, Ross, Garcia, Lute, Artur), Ilia (Fiora, Farina), Etruria (Pent, Louise, Erk, Priscilla, Raven, Lucius, Canas), Western Isles (Dart, Geitz, Renault, Hawkeye), Bern (Heath, Vaida), Nabata (Karel, Karla, Isadora, Harken), Darkling Woods (Myrrh).

Set pieces: "Arcadia" (defend without killing dragons), "The Archive" (the Scouring truth), and the Ninian/Ysolde midpoint. Leila's echo appears here.

### Act III — Beyond the Gate (Ch. 22–30 + Final)
The armies go *into* the seam. Floating ruins, drowned cities, a world made of exiled memory. Echo-heavy. Athos joins as an echo. Nils is found holding a light in the dark. Ysolde's turn comes when she sees the Hollow Throng. Final chapter: "The Door and the Keeper."

## 8. Gameplay Pillars (TMGC-inspired)

- **Higher numbers.** Stats run hotter than vanilla so skills and matchups have room to matter.
- **Every playable unit has a personal skill.** With 77 units and roughly nine cavaliers, nobody is allowed to be interchangeable.
- **Varied objectives:** seize, rout, defend, survive, escape, rescue, split maps, and boss kills with a timer.
- **Telegraphed reinforcements.** Reinforcements never ambush-spawn and act on the same turn. The seam glows the turn before something comes through.
- **Bosses with kits.** Echo bosses get skills drawn from their original game identity.
- **Seam tiles (new mechanic, planned):** map tiles that pulse. Enemies can come through them, and a unit standing on one can seal it (using the Dragon Vein framework).
- **Supports and base conversations**, with cross-roster pairings as a feature and not an afterthought.

## 9. QoL (current and planned)

Current (from the FE8 Skill System base): enemy danger zone (Select), HP bars, battle stats with animations off, hold L to toggle animations, Casual Mode, talking or supporting without ending the unit's turn, weapon rank shown on level-up, negative weight/crit display, damage numbers in battle.

Planned: growths visible on the stat screen, fast text by default, skippable world map, convoy access from the prep screen and on-map.

## 10. Cross-Roster Support Pairs (starter list)

- **Hector × Ephraim**: two lance-and-axe meatheads who become best friends instantly. Structural damage.
- **Lyn × Eirika**: sword lords with opposite relationships to their own nobility.
- **Eliwood × Knoll**: the gentle lord who would have *understood* Lyon.
- **Canas × Knoll**: dark-magic nerds comparing notes.
- **Matthew × Colm**, **Legault × Rennac**: the thieves' union.
- **Sain × Forde**, **Kent × Kyle**: the cavalier mirror. Everyone at camp stops to watch.
- **Serra × L'Arachel**: two clerics, each convinced the other is the sidekick.
- **Farina × Tana**: pegasus rivalry, then friendship.
- **Renault × Moulder**: two priests, very different pasts.
- **Raven × Innes**: grump summit.
- **Ninian × Myrrh**: the heart of the dragon plot.
- **Nino × Ewan**: magic prodigies.
- **Oswin × Gilliam**: the quietest support in the game. Six lines total.

## 11. Technical Notes

- Base: FE8U + [FE8 Skill System](https://github.com/FireEmblemUniverse/SkillSystem_FE8) buildfiles, assembled with ColorzCore.
- FE7 assets (portraits, eventually animations) are ported from a user-supplied FE7U ROM at build time; nothing from either game is distributed in this repo.
- Until FE7 class animations are ported, FE7 veterans use FE8 class animations as placeholders (Knight Lord ≈ Paladin, Great Lord Hector ≈ General/Great Knight, Blade Lord ≈ Swordmaster).
