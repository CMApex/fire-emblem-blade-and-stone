# Fire Emblem: Blade & Stone — Design Bible (v2)

*FE8 (Sacred Stones) buildfile hack. FE7 and FE8 veterans, one original story, roughly a year after both games.*

Internal working doc. Load-bearing sections: **Premise, Canon Baseline, Rules of the Crossover, Ysolde, Echoes, the third-option ending.** Everything else is negotiable.

Status tags used below:
- **[OPEN]**: undecided. Nat's call. Don't fill in silently.
- **[PROPOSED]**: a default Claude would use if forced to pick. Not decided.
- **[NEEDS SCRIPT]**: sounds right on paper. Has to prove itself in actual scenes.
- **[UNTESTED]**: mechanical idea with no playtest behind it.
- **[CUT IF SLOW]**: first thing to go if an act drags.

v2 is a revision of v1 after a canon audit. See §13 for the change log.

## 1. Premise

In the same year, on two continents across the Gray Sea, two wars ended.

In **Elibe**, Eliwood, Hector and Lyn killed Nergal on Valor. Nils went back through the Dragons' Gate and sealed it from the far side. Ninian stayed with Eliwood. She knew what that meant: Elibe's air has changed too much for a dragon's strength ever to come back.

In **Magvel**, Eirika and Ephraim defeated Fomortiis. Four of the five Sacred Stones were destroyed during the war. The fifth, Rausten's, survived. In the final battle it became the prison of the Demon King's soul, and his body was destroyed.

Neither continent knows the two victories touched the same old wound.

The wound is the **seam**, a damaged layer between worlds. The Scouring and the Ending Winter tore it a thousand years ago. The Dragons' Gate is a road *through* it to the world where the dragons went (§3.2). Two centuries after the Scouring, Magvel bound Fomortiis with five Sacred Stones. Those Stones also braced Magvel's side of the seam, whether or not their makers knew it [OPEN, see §12.2].

Now four Stones are gone, and the last one is already full. The Gate at Valor is sealed from the far side. One year on, the pressure has nowhere to go. Fractures open at the ruined Stone shrines and under Valor, and then the Gray Sea starts glowing at night.

Core idea, *for the writers, not for characters to say*: both victories were right, and both have consequences.

## 2. Tone

Serious, with real stakes. Not grimdark.

- The world is in real danger, and choices cost something. People we love get hurt. The dead come back only as Echoes, and Echoes don't fix anything.
- **No shock deaths of playable characters.** Loss comes from the past: what the wars already took, and what the Echoes can't give back.
- Warmth and comedy live in supports, base conversations and the quiet stretches. Comedy is **not** a pressure valve after every dark beat. FE7 and FE8 both let sadness sit, and so do we.
- Villains have reasons. Ysolde's grievance is real (§4).
- Register: FE7/FE8's earnest script voice. Direct emotional language, titles where rank matters, melodrama played straight, and people who fail to say the right thing. TMGC's sincerity. Not Fates gag density. No Marvel quips after tragedy.

### 2.1 Writing rules for scene text
1. **The bible can know the theme. Characters rarely say it.** No closing aphorisms.
2. No modern therapy vocabulary. No academic thesis phrasing. No "you're right, but..." debate ping-pong.
3. "Genocide" is fine in this document. In dialogue, use period-native words: *slaughter, purge, hunted, driven out, the Scouring*.
4. Humor comes from personality clashes, not punchline density.
5. Scenes are allowed to end unresolved.
6. Every veteran is a person, not their crossover joke (see §10).

## 3. Canon Baseline and Rules of the Crossover

### 3.1 Canon facts we build on (checked against the scripts)
- **Rausten's Stone survived FE8** and holds Fomortiis's soul. His body was destroyed. (FE8 Final: *"The Demon King's soul has been bound once more."* / *"We've destroyed the Demon King's body."*) Four Stones are gone: Renais, Frelia, Jehanna, Grado.
- **Fomortiis is an individual demon** with a soul and a body. He is not a collective and not a dragon.
- **Timeline:** the Scouring was about 1,000 years before FE7. Fomortiis fell about 800 years before FE8. **They are separate events**, about two centuries apart.
- **The Gate leads to another inhabited world.** Nils describes it as a place where the dragons were stable and happy, with humans there too. The dragons went *there*. They were not dumped into a void.
- **Ninian's fading has a canon cause:** Elibe's changed air means her strength will never return (FE7 Final). The Gate closing didn't cause it. It removed her road to the world where she could recover.
- **Arcadia** has had peaceful human–dragon coexistence for centuries. That proof must survive this story.
- **Vigarde** died of illness *before* the invasion. Lyon reanimated the body.
- Orson's wife is **Monica**. What Orson kept was an animated corpse, not her.
- **Valor** is off Lycia's **southern** coast.
- **Desmond** is alive one year after FE7. He dies much later.
- **Nergal as Ninian and Nils's father** is strongly implied (19xx, plus the corrected Aenir line). We treat it as true, but it is *not* common knowledge in-world.

### 3.2 Cosmology (revised)
```
Elibe  ──►  the seam (damaged interstitial layer)  ──►  the dragons' world
                      ▲
Magvel's Sacred Stones braced it from this side (4 of 5 now gone)
```
- **The seam** is a scar between worlds, left by the Scouring and the Ending Winter. It holds stranded fragments, failed crossings, residue, and memory without whole identity. The Gate crosses it; the seam is not a place anyone was sent to live. What the seam *physically* is (transit layer, wound, pressure system) is **[OPEN §12.3]**.
- **The Hollow Throng** is the aggregate danger inside the seam: corrupted residue, fragments, things warped by centuries of pressure. It may carry traces of dragons, humans, and Fomortiis's influence. It is **not** "exiled dragons gone mad," and it is **not** Fomortiis. Dragons are not the source of corruption; the story rejects that equation.
- **Fomortiis and the seam** are related but not the same thing. **[PROPOSED]** Fomortiis found and fed on power leaking through Magvel's side of the seam. Fighting him taught the ancients how to make Stones that could bind that kind of power, and that incidentally braced the wound.
- **Why now:** in the same year, four stabilizers were destroyed and the Gate was sealed from the far side. The load redistributed. Old scars reopened, first at the Stone shrines and under Valor, then out on the Gray Sea.
- **The Rausten Stone** now carries a burden it was never meant to carry alone. It's the Act I pressure point: dreams, heat, hairline cracks. **Fomortiis is not free at the start of the game.** Any crisis threatens his prison. It doesn't quietly undo FE8's ending.

### 3.3 Rules
1. **Time:** about one year after FE7 and FE8. Everyone is a veteran. Hector is Marquess of Ostia; Ephraim is King of Renais. Both have governments, duties and absences that need covering (§7.5).
2. **Geography:** one world, with Elibe and Magvel across the Gray Sea. Valor stays off southern Lycia, so **Magvel lies roughly south to southeast of Elibe** on our world map. The sea is historically near-impassable. Why exactly is **[OPEN §12.4]**.
3. **FE7 continuity:** the Eliwood/Ninian paired ending. **Lyn unpaired**: she went back to Sacae and entrusted Caelin to Ostia. Whether Eliwood and Ninian are married yet, and whether Roy is born, is **[OPEN §12.9]**. **[PROPOSED]** They're married, Roy is not born, and Roy stays offscreen either way.
4. **FE8 continuity is composite, not additive.** Events and characters from both routes can be used where they're compatible. Where they conflict, Blade & Stone picks one version and sticks to it. All recruitable FE8 units survived. Orson and Glen are dead.
5. **Lyon rule [PROPOSED, needs Nat's sign-off]:** Lyon was genuinely corrupted and increasingly possessed. He also kept intermittent agency, jealousy, self-deception, and moments of real choice. Neither "innocent puppet" nor "secret villain." Both were partly true at different times. His Echo shows fragments of each.
6. **No time travel, and no summoning of living alternate versions.** Crossings and Echoes come from the damaged seam.
7. **Echoes** are fragments of the dead, never resurrections, and incomplete by nature (§6).
8. **Ninian and Myrrh** are the emotional axis of the dragon plot. They come from different histories and different dragon traditions. Don't collapse them into one mythology.
9. **FE6 compatibility is [OPEN §12.1].** It constrains the ending hard, so decide it before Act III is written.

## 4. The Antagonist: Ysolde of Arcadia

A human scholar raised in Arcadia. Patient, kind to her followers, genuinely moved by the heroes. She never gloats. She's wrong in a way that follows from love.

### 4.1 Her grievance
Arcadia keeps accounts that human histories minimized or erased. After the Ending Winter weakened the dragons, human armies hunted down dragons who could no longer hold their true forms. Ysolde calls the Scouring a genocide, and the archive gives her plenty of reason to. How the war *began* is still contested, in canon and in the story. The heroes can't beat her argument with one gotcha fact. They have to **accept the atrocity without accepting her solution.**

### 4.2 Seren (her dragon mother)
**[PROPOSED]** Seren is an old Arcadian dragon who has been weakening for *years*. Elibe is survivable for dragons, and Arcadia proves it, but it's not the world their bodies were made for. When Ysolde sees Ninian failing so much faster, she takes it as proof that access to the other world matters. How long Seren has been ill, and what Ysolde *knows* versus *believes* about the cause, is **[OPEN §12.7]**. **Never** "every Arcadian dragon started dying when the Gate closed."

### 4.3 Her goal
Tear the seam open permanently so any dragon who needs to can cross, and make sure nobody can ever wall them in again. She is right about the injustice and wrong that unlimited rupture will heal it.

### 4.4 Her offer to Ninian (midpoint)
Not "join me or die." More like: *there is a road back to your strength. You gave it up once for love, and I can give you that choice again.* It tests Ninian's old decision without saying it was a mistake. [NEEDS SCRIPT]

### 4.5 Her turn happens in stages, not all at once
1. She sees evidence against her model.
2. She rationalizes it.
3. The rupture hurts someone she meant to save, ideally a dragon or Seren.
4. She tries to control the opening and learns it can't tell rescue from destruction.
5. She chooses repair over winning.

It has to cost her something. No deathbed redemption is needed, just a believable collapse of certainty.

### 4.6 Her allies (working)
- **Brother Maelis** (Magvel): an excommunicated Rausten priest who reads the Stones' destruction as a sign. He leads **the Unwritten**, people the war took everything from. He wants the Rausten Stone, and his reason is **[OPEN]**.
- **Desmond of Bern** (Elibe, background): funds "archaeology" on Valor for local, selfish reasons. Leverage against Lycia, weapons, succession politics, Zephiel. He is **not** the hidden architect of anything.
- **Kaelen Reed** (Elibe): a Black Fang splinter leader hunting Nino and Jaffar. Petty and personal, a mid-Act foil. [CUT IF SLOW]
- **How Ysolde knows about Magvel at all** is **[OPEN §12.5]**. Arcadia can't just conveniently hold full knowledge of another continent.

## 5. The Third Option: a door, not a wall

Arcadia proves coexistence is possible. The ending's answer to "seal it forever" versus "tear it open" is a **regulated passage**: a door rather than a wall, repaired inside the seam.

- Ninian and Myrrh help design and attune it, **voluntarily**. They are not batteries, not eternal jailers, and not sacrifices.
- What the door *costs* is **[OPEN §12.12]**. It needs a real constraint.
- **If FE6 compatibility is kept,** the door is hidden and narrow: deliberate passage, not migration. Arcadia stays secret, and Elibe doesn't normalize a return of dragons. **If it's dropped,** Blade & Stone is openly an alternate continuity after FE7, and the door can change the world.
- **Nils:** he got home and sealed the Gate, exactly as FE7 ends. Months later, when the seam started failing, he went *into* it from the far side to hold a rupture shut. That's where the heroes find him in Act III. He's there because he chose to go in, not because he never made it home.

## 6. Echoes

**Rule:** an Echo is not a ghost therapist and not the whole dead person. It's a surviving shape: a desire, fear, promise, obsession, habit, or memory. It often lacks the exact part the living character most wants to hear. Echoes should usually leave a scene with **less certainty and more truth.** They are never an automatic closure device.

### 7.3 Act III — Through the Seam (Ch. 22–30 + Final)
Into the seam: drowned places, broken crossings, fragments. Echo-heavy. Athos plays for a short, limited stretch. Nils is found holding a rupture shut (§5). Ysolde's staged turn (§4.5). Final chapter: "The Door." Act III never enters the dragons' actual world. It's all the damaged layer *between*. **[OPEN]** whether the epilogue shows the far side at all.

### 7.4 Rausten Stone through the acts [PROPOSED]
- Act I: dreams and heat around it.
- Chapter 6: the first crack.
- Act II: Maelis moves on it.
- Act III: the condition it's in shapes the final repair.

Carrying or moving it is dangerous, because Fomortiis's soul is still inside. It's not a generic magic battery.

### 7.5 Big-cast realism
With 77 veterans, not everyone is free to join the same expedition at once. People have countries, armies, families and rebuilding to do. Side parties and staggered joins make the world feel bigger than the army. "Not deployable yet" doesn't mean "forgotten." **[OPEN §12.10]** who governs Ostia and Renais while Hector and Ephraim campaign abroad. Oswin holds Ostia in Ch. 1's ending for now, which conflicts with Oswin joining the army and needs resolving.

## 8. Gameplay Pillars (TMGC-inspired)

- **Veterans feel like veterans.** Promoted classes from the start, higher stat ceilings, stronger enemy baselines. Growth is mostly specialization, skills and gear, not relearning skills they canonically already have. Late joiners arrive at a believable power level.
- **Higher numbers**, so skills and matchups have room to matter.
- **Every playable unit has a personal skill.** Nobody is interchangeable, and there are nine-ish cavaliers to tell apart.
- **Varied objectives:** seize, rout, defend, survive, escape, rescue, split maps, timed boss kills.
- **Telegraphed reinforcements.** Nothing spawns and acts on the same turn.
- **Seam tiles [UNTESTED]** with distinct visual states: dormant scar, unstable (opens next turn), open, sealed or attuned, and corrupted (Fomortiis or Throng influence). Built on the Dragon Vein / trap framework.
- **Rausten Stone on the map [UNTESTED]:** suppresses seam activity near it, and carrying it is a risk.
- **Bosses with kits.** Echo bosses draw skills from their original-game identity.
- **Supports and base conversations**, with cross-roster pairs as a feature.

## 9. QoL

**Current** (from the FE8 Skill System base): danger zone (Select), HP bars, battle stats with animations off, hold L to toggle animations, Casual Mode, talking or supporting doesn't end the turn, weapon rank on level-up, negative weight and crit display, damage numbers.

**Planned:** growths on the stat screen, fast text by default, skippable world map, convoy on the prep screen and on-map.

## 11. Technical Notes

- Base: FE8U plus [FE8 Skill System](https://github.com/FireEmblemUniverse/SkillSystem_FE8) buildfiles, assembled with ColorzCore.
- FE7 assets are ported at build time from the user's own FE7U ROM. No game data lives in the repo.
- FE7 veterans use FE8 class animations as placeholders until FE7 animations are ported.
- Per-chapter "player leader" hook: FE8 hardcodes Eirika. Ours reads a per-chapter table, which is needed for any chapter she isn't in.
- Playtesting uses a headless emulator bot (tools/playtest) that plays real turns.

## 13. Change Log (v1 → v2)

### Canon corrections
- Rausten's Stone survives and holds Fomortiis's soul. v1 said all five were shattered.
- The Scouring (~1,000 years before FE7) and Fomortiis's defeat (~800 years before FE8) are separate events. v1 made them one seal from one event.
- The Gate leads to the dragons' actual world. The seam is the layer it crosses, not where they were exiled.
- Ninian's fading comes from Elibe's changed air, per FE7. v1 blamed the Gate closing.
- Arcadia's dragons don't start dying when the Gate closes. Seren's illness is a long decline.
- Fomortiis stays an individual demon. The Hollow Throng is separate.
- "Both FE8 routes happened" is replaced with a composite-continuity rule plus a Lyon rule.
- Nils got home and sealed the Gate. His time in the seam comes later and by choice.
- Valor is off *southern* Lycia, so Magvel sits south to southeast.
- Caelin is Ostia-administered after Lyn's unpaired ending. Ch. 7 is renamed.
- Vigarde's Echo is about fatherhood and succession, not guilt for his corpse's orders.
- Monika is corrected to **Monica**. Monica and Orson get separate Echoes.
- Desmond's role is narrowed to local self-interest.

### Creative changes
- Ysolde's grievance is now strongly evidenced rather than stated as omniscient fact. Her turn happens in five stages.
- Her offer to Ninian is reframed as giving back a choice, not a threat.
- The Rausten Stone becomes a recurring pressure point in the story and on the map.
- Echo guardrails added: incomplete, not closure devices.
- Scene-writing rules added (§2.1). Comedy isn't a pressure valve.
- Support pairs get an "unexpected discovery" rule, and the archetype shorthand is corrected (Sain ≠ Forde, and so on).
- Veterans-feel-like-veterans pillar added. Seam-tile states specified.
- Open Questions section added. Status tags added.

### Existing script lines that now conflict (not changed yet, awaiting OK)
- **World-map intro** (`BS_WMIntro`): *"The Sacred Stones that bound him... are gone, shattered, one by one"* is wrong now. Needs to say four of five, and the last one is holding him.
- **Prologue ending, Legault**: *"sailing east of Lycia, near the Dread Isle"* should be *south* of Lycia.
- **Ch. 1 ending**: *"Oswin, Ostia is yours until I'm back"* conflicts with Oswin being a deployable unit. It depends on §12.10.
- Everything else in the Prologue and Ch. 1 checks out against the revised canon. Ephraim's list of shrines names only the four destroyed Stones, and Ninian "sleeping more" fits the correct cause of her fading.
